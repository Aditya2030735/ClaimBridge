What we do

Everything Billing. Done Right.

We handle the full revenue cycle — from eligibility verification to payment posting — so nothing slips through the cracks and you get paid faster.

Eligibility & Benefits Verification

Verify patient coverage before every appointment. Avoid denials from inactive or invalid policies.

Claim Submission & Tracking

Accurate, scrubbed claims submitted promptly. We manage resubmissions and continuous follow-up until paid.

Denial Management & Appeals

Systematic follow-up on aging claims. We work directly with insurers to resolve denials and recover revenue.

Payment Posting (ERA/EOB)

Post ERA and EOB payments accurately with daily reconciliation to capture every dollar of revenue.

Prior Authorization Assistance

We submit and track authorization requests, completing all required documentation to reduce treatment delays.

Provider Credentialing

Enroll new providers with insurance networks, manage re-credentialing, and ensure ongoing compliance.

Patient Calling Services

Proactively call patients to confirm or fill appointments. Decrease no-shows and improve chair utilization.

Fee Schedule Maintenance

Maintain up-to-date payer fee schedules to support consistent billing and accurate reimbursement every time.
